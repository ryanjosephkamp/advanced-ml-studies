\section{Conclusion}

On the raw Iris measurements, only the Gaussian kernel, at one fixed width that is small next to the distances between the means of neighboring species, largely separated versicolor from the other two species (\N{gauss_p3_mean} against \N{d3_p3_mean} to \N{dot_p3_mean}). The polynomial kernels underfit, with little sign of overfitting apart from degree 3 on Problem 3, and in the added comparison, standardizing the features lifts them on that problem to \N{s_d2_p3_std} and \N{s_d3_p3_std}, still below the Gaussian's \N{s_gauss_p3_std}. The training share between 50\% and 90\% does not change the observed average accuracy over problem--kernel pairs by more than \N{all13_span}, and the favorable single split used in the original study should not be read as the expected accuracy.
