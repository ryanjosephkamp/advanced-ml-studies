\section{Limitations}

There are five small tabular data sets, SVMs only, and a 70/30 split per draw rather than nested cross-validation. Test sets have \N{z:n_test:iris} to \N{z:n_test:breast_cancer} rows. The tuning grids were fixed in advance and are narrow for the polynomial kernels. The scalers are fit on all training rows before the inner folds, which is a small leak within the training set, and inner cross-validation scores plain accuracy even for Glass, whose headline is balanced accuracy. The 95\% intervals are bootstrap intervals over draws. Equivalence calls are per comparison, use 95\% intervals (stricter than the usual 90\%), and have no multiplicity adjustment. The replication intervals are not adjusted across data sets and use the report's one split. The Iris results in the replication use the older data file. The plotted tail bound is the Gaussian one; Achlioptas proves a lower-tail bound for Eq.~3 and Eq.~4 only in a looser form, and neither covers $s > 3$. The study does not test very sparse projections in high dimensions, which is Li, Hastie, and Church's setting. A libsvm iteration cap of $10^8$ was reached by \N{z:capped} of \N{z:n_fits_m} million fits.
