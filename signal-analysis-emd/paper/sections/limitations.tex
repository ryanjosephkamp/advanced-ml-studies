\section{Limitations}

The pipeline uses three or five channels, five bands, two-second windows, and no spatial filtering, by design, so these are not competitive brain--computer interface (BCI) results; published methods for BCI IV 2a use all 22 channels and score far higher \cite{ang2012}. EEGMAT's classes are two recordings made in a fixed order with an imbalance of \N{c:eegmat_rest_pct}\% rest, the channels used are frontal only, and its held-out result rests mostly on Fp1 and Fp2, while the alpha change is expected to be strongest over parietal and occipital sites, which were not used. BCI IV 2a has 9 subjects, so intervals are wide and may still be too narrow, and no paired comparison can pass Holm. Within-subject permutation tests are approximate because the models are not refit, and EEG MMI has only \N{c:mmi_win_min} to \N{c:mmi_win_max} windows per subject. The held-out standardization is transductive: it needs the new subject's unlabeled session. The zero floor (Section~\ref{sec:design}) is also taken over all windows, held-out ones included, though it uses no labels and changes only zero values. The spectra intervals are pointwise.
