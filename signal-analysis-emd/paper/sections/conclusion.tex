\section{Conclusion}

The original chance results are consistent with the design, which averaged away most of the lateralized motor signal; padding artifacts and, likely, outliers inflated the clustering scores. With channels kept separate, EEG classes separate modestly on held-out subjects, and on EEGMAT only with the recording-order and frontal-polar caveats. EMD features come close to band power at best, and none beats it here after Holm's correction.
