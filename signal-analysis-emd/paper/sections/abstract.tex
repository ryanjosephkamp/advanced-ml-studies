Empirical mode decomposition (EMD) splits a signal into intrinsic mode functions (IMFs) that can serve as features. A coursework pipeline applied EMD to two-second electroencephalography (EEG) windows from three public data sets, two of motor imagery and one of mental arithmetic, and classified and clustered the IMF features under three distances. It scored at chance on motor imagery, and its highest clustering scores came from zero padding in the IMF rows and, likely, from outliers. This paper corrects three bugs, reruns that baseline, and asks a sharper question: does EMD add anything over a plain spectral baseline on people the model has not seen? With channels kept separate, band power classifies held-out subjects above chance, with balanced accuracy \N{sm:eeg_mmi:F1_ch:lda:heldout:subject} on EEG Motor Movement/Imagery (EEG MMI, chance 0.50), \N{sm:bci_civ_2a:F1_ch:lda:heldout:subject} on BCI Competition IV data set 2a (BCI IV 2a, chance 0.25), and \N{sm:eeg_ma:F1_ch:lda:heldout:subject} on EEG During Mental Arithmetic Tasks (EEGMAT, chance 0.50) when each held-out subject's features are standardized over its own unlabeled windows, and \N{sm:eeg_mmi:F1_ch:lda:heldout:none}, \N{sm:bci_civ_2a:F1_ch:lda:heldout:none}, and \N{sm:eeg_ma:F1_ch:lda:heldout:none} without standardizing. Findings (the first four for linear discriminant analysis on held-out subjects): (1.) with channels kept separate, Hilbert band power from the IMFs scores within about \N{mag:c:hilb_min} of band power on average, though no equivalence test was run; (2.) with channels kept separate, the IMF features themselves score lower; (3.) averaging and z-scoring the channels, as the original pipeline did, costs \N{c:avgcost_min} to \N{c:avgcost_max} (\N{c:avgcost_none_min} to \N{c:avgcost_none_max} without standardizing); (4.) on EEGMAT, whose classes are two recordings made in a fixed order, the held-out result leans on the frontal-polar channels and may partly reflect recording order, so it does not by itself show a cortical effect; and (5.) clusters do not follow the classes; some original-feature runs follow row counts, and \N{c:avg_link_95} of \N{c:avg_link_total} average-linkage runs show an imbalance consistent with outliers.
